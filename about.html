// the story

A little about me

I'm Vishnuraj KJ — a Senior UI/UX Designer from Kochi with over 5 years of experience turning complex problems into clean, usable, and visually compelling digital products.

My career spans SaaS platforms, enterprise tools, health tech, e-commerce, and AI-powered products. Whether it's a zero-to-one product or a full redesign, I approach every project with the same curiosity: what does the user actually need, and how do we make it feel effortless?

I currently lead design at Alignminds Technologies, where I've shipped 20+ products, established design systems, and integrated AI-assisted workflows into the day-to-day design process.

When I'm not pushing pixels, I'm usually exploring design trends, experimenting with new tools, or questioning why a button is that particular shade of grey.

// how I work

Principles I design by

01

Users first, always

Every decision starts with the user. Research, empathy, and testing aren't extras — they're the foundation of every project I take on.

02

Simplicity is hard work

The best interfaces are ones that feel obvious. Getting there takes multiple iterations, ruthless editing, and the discipline to remove anything that doesn't earn its place.

03

Design is a team sport

Great products come from close collaboration with engineers, PMs, and stakeholders. I stay involved through build, not just at handoff.

04

Details change everything

Spacing, motion, micro-interactions — these aren't polish. They're what separates a good product from one people actually love using.
